---
name: grill-with-docs
description: A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design, which also creates docs (ADRs, DDRs and glossary) as we go. Use it when the user wants a plan, design or idea stress-tested inside a repo, or says grill, grill me or grill with docs.
---

Call the Skill tool three times, for "grilling", "domain-modeling" and "design-decision". Terms go to the glossary. Decisions about the product as a whole (its purpose, its audience, a founding concept) and hard-to-reverse choices go to ADRs. Every settled rule for how something is built goes to the project's design decision register as a DDR, before anything is built from it.
