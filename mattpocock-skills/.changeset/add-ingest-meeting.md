Add the `ingest-meeting` skill, which turns a meeting transcript into the project's records. It saves a dated note in `docs/meetings/` (or where `docs/agents/domain.md` names), extracts every candidate DDR, ADR, glossary term, ticket, question and conflict with a verbatim quote and timestamp, checks the quotes against the transcript with a bundled script, checks each candidate against the glossary, records and tracker, and settles them with the user in a grilling, ideally one round and conflicts first, before `domain-modeling` and `design-decision` write anything. A conflict with a recorded decision goes in only as the user resolves it. A decision agreed in the meeting stays `proposed` until the user accepts it. A loose transcript file is deleted once its note holds the whole transcript, and one already noted is deleted rather than ingested twice. It is model-invoked, so a video digest can hand over to it. The skill is six short steps that call `domain-modeling`, `grilling` and `design-decision` by the Skill tool and follow their conventions, rather than restating them.
